Privacy Policy

Ronko: Snoring Recorder · Last updated September 27, 2026

Ronko records the sounds of your night — snoring, sleep talk and other noises — so you can listen to them in the morning. It works on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no server that stores your recordings, and we don't sell or share your data.

Microphone

While a night is recording, Ronko listens through the microphone, including when the screen is locked. It keeps only short clips of moments that are louder than your room and discards the silence. Each clip is labelled (snoring, talk, other) by Apple's built-in sound classifier running on your iPhone. Audio is never uploaded, and Ronko never listens when you have not started a night.

What stays on your device

  • Your recorded clips and their labels, times and loudness.
  • A loudness value for each minute of the night.
  • Evening notes you add to a night (for example “late meal”).
  • Your answers from the first-run questions and settings such as sensitivity and how long clips are kept.

This information is stored only in Ronko's own storage on your iPhone. Clips older than your “keep clips for” setting are deleted automatically.

Sharing

A clip leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself with the share button, to the app or person you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Ronko receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs; it never receives your recordings, labels or notes.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow it, Ronko schedules one local notification a day before the trial ends. No push server is involved.

Analytics and tracking

Ronko does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Not a medical device

Ronko records and plays back sounds. It does not assess, diagnose or monitor any health condition.

Deleting your data

Delete any clip from its screen, choose a shorter “keep clips for” period in Settings, or delete Ronko from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Ronko is not directed at children under 13.
