Ronko · Snoring Recorder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

Ronko listens to your bedroom only after you press Start tonight. The sound is analysed on your iPhone, the clips stay on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded to us or to any server we run. There is no account to create.

1 · The microphone

When a night is running, Ronko reads the microphone, also with the screen locked (it uses the iOS audio background mode for this). It first learns how loud the room is, then keeps short clips only of moments that rise above that level; silence is discarded. Apple's on-device SoundAnalysis classifier labels each clip as snoring, sleep talk or other sound. Ronko does not record outside a night you started.

2 · What is stored, and where

  • The clips (audio files), with label, time and loudness, in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • A loudness reading for each minute of the night, and the evening notes you tick (late meal, alcohol, blocked nose and so on).
  • Your settings, such as sensitivity and how many days clips are kept (default 30, then they are deleted automatically).

This data is not sent anywhere by Ronko. It may be included in your own device or iCloud backups according to your iOS settings.

3 · Sharing a clip

The share button hands one clip to the iOS share sheet. It leaves the phone only if you pick an app or person, and from then on that recipient's rules apply.

4 · Purchases and the Adapty service

Payment is handled by Apple. To manage Ronko Pro, the app uses the Adapty SDK, which creates an anonymous profile and receives your purchase status, product and transaction information, device and app version details, and a few anonymous onboarding or paywall events. Adapty does not receive your audio, clips or notes, and we do not ask for your name or email in the app. See Adapty's own privacy policy for how it processes this data.

5 · What we do not do

No advertising identifier, no tracking across other companies' apps or sites, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, no sale of data.

6 · Deleting

Delete a single clip from its screen, shorten the keep period in Settings, or remove Ronko from the iPhone to erase everything stored by the app. Adapty's anonymous purchase profile can be deleted on request by email.

7 · Not a medical device

Ronko records and plays back sounds. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. If you worry about your breathing during sleep, talk to a doctor.

8 · Children, changes, contact

Ronko is not aimed at children under 13. Changes to this policy are posted here with a new date. Questions: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
