What Ronko is

Ronko is a night sound journal: it records moments louder than your room and labels them on your iPhone. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.

Recording others

If other people sleep in the room, let them know you are recording. You are responsible for following the recording laws where you live.
