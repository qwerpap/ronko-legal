Nothing was recorded

Check that the microphone is allowed in Settings › Ronko, that the phone is within an arm's reach of your pillow, and try High sensitivity in Ronko's settings.

Too many noise clips

Choose Low sensitivity — useful with a fan, air conditioner or a partner in the room.

Battery

A full night of listening uses battery. Keep the phone on the charger overnight.
