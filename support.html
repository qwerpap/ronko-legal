Write to us

netkaktakto@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

Nothing got recorded

Check Settings › Ronko › Microphone. Put the phone within reach of your pillow, keep it plugged in, and try High sensitivity in Ronko's settings.

Too many clips of noise

Switch sensitivity to Low. A fan, air conditioner or a restless partner raises the room level, so Ronko picks up more.

A wrong label

Open the clip and change its label. Clips you don't need can be deleted there as well.

Battery and storage

Listening all night draws power, so charge the phone. Silence is skipped, so one night takes only a few megabytes; set a shorter keep period in Settings to free space.

Restore or cancel Pro

Restore: Settings in Ronko, then Restore purchases (tied to your Apple ID). Cancel a subscription: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Ronko.
